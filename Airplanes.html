Training

After graduating from university, I began flight lessons and built more than 60 hours of dual and solo flight time, mostly around Delta, BC.

Aircraft

The Cessna 152 is a classic training aircraft because it is stable, cost-effective, straightforward to maintain, and forgiving enough to make it a useful platform for learning fundamentals.

Systems

  • Aircraft mechanical and electrical systems.
  • Radio communication and flight operations.
  • Maneuvering techniques and emergency procedures.

Why It Belongs Here

Flying makes the same systems mindset behind RF, satellites, and maritime engineering feel immediate. Inputs, constraints, failure modes, and human decisions all become part of one operating system.
